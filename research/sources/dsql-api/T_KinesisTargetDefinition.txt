KinesisTargetDefinition
Kinesis stream target configuration.
      Contents
                  roleArn
               The ARN of the IAM role that grants permission to write to the Kinesis stream. This can
         be a standard role (arn:aws:iam::account-id:role/role-name) or a role with a
         path prefix (arn:aws:iam::account-id:role/service-role/role-name), such as
         roles auto-created by the console.
               Type: String
               Length Constraints: Minimum length of 20. Maximum length of 2048.
               Pattern: arn:aws(-[^:]+)?:iam::[0-9]{12}:role(/[a-zA-Z0-9+=,.@_-]+)+
               Required: Yes
                  streamArn
               The ARN of the Kinesis stream.
               Type: String
               Length Constraints: Minimum length of 1. Maximum length of 2048.
               Pattern: arn:aws[a-zA-Z-]*:kinesis:[a-z0-9-]*:[0-9]{12}:stream/[a-zA-Z0-9+=,.@_/\-]+
               Required: Yes
